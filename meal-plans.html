LEARN THE BASICS

What each macro does

🥚

Protein

Supports muscle repair and growth. Spread protein-rich foods across your meals.

Sources: eggs, dairy, dal, beans, soy, fish, chicken
🍚

Carbohydrates

Your body’s main training fuel. Prefer fibre-rich and minimally processed sources.

Sources: rice, roti, oats, fruit, potatoes, legumes
🥜

Fats

Support hormones, cells, and absorption of fat-soluble vitamins.

Sources: nuts, seeds, oils, avocado, dairy, fish
🥦

Fibre & water

Support digestion, fullness, hydration, and overall health.

Sources: vegetables, fruit, whole grains, pulses, water

How this calculator works

Calories: Estimates resting energy with the Mifflin–St Jeor equation, applies an activity factor, then uses a modest 300 kcal goal adjustment.

Protein: Uses 1.6 g per kg of body weight as a simple active-adult target.

Fats: Uses 25% of estimated calories. Remaining calories are assigned to carbohydrates.

Learn more: Mifflin–St Jeor equation, USDA macronutrients, CDC healthy weight loss.
Important: These calculations and meal examples are educational estimates for generally healthy adults. Needs vary with health conditions, medications, pregnancy, food allergies, culture, and training demands. Consult a registered dietitian or healthcare professional for individual advice.
